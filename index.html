What you get

Everything between the print and the scan

Six things Scanova handles so a QR Code stops being a one-way barcode and starts being a channel you can measure and improve.

Dynamic codes

Edit content after printing

Change the destination link, swap an offer, or fix a typo — the printed code never has to be reprinted.

Custom design

Codes that match your brand

Add your logo, colors, eye patterns, and frames so the code looks like it belongs on your material.

Tracking & analytics

See every scan as it happens

Time, location, device, and OS for every scan — enough detail to know which placement is actually working.

Mobile landing pages

A destination, not just a link

Build a mobile-optimized page with menus, forms, videos, or RSVPs — no developer required.

Lead generation

Turn scans into contacts

Collect names and emails right at the point of scan, and export the list whenever you need it.

APIs & integrations

Fits your existing stack

Generate and manage codes programmatically, or connect to Zapier, Google Analytics, and your ad platforms.

Setup

Live in three steps

Most teams have a working, trackable QR Code inside of ten minutes — no design tool or developer needed.

01

Pick a QR Code type

Website, document, business card, Wi-Fi, event, menu — choose from 26 categories built for a specific use case.

02

Design and connect it

Add your brand colors and logo, then point it at a URL, a landing page, or a file you upload directly.

03

Print, publish, and watch it work

Download in PNG, SVG, or PDF, put it out in the world, and track scans from the same dashboard.
